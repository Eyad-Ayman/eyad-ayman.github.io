01 — About the Artist

That's Me

جامد صح؟

Hey there!

I'm Eyad Ayman Hemdan, a 20-year-old from Giza, Egypt, deeply immersed in graphic design, video editing, and 3D art. My journey into the creative world began in 2017 when I first started learning Photoshop, and by 2019, I had officially started exploring graphic design and video editing.

Armed with Adobe's powerful tools like Photoshop, Illustrator, and Premiere Pro, I craft visuals and videos that captivate and inspire. Whether it's a striking graphic, a dynamic video edit, or pushing the boundaries of 3D art using Blender, my goal is always to create impactful experiences.

While studying Marketing at Cairo University's Faculty of Commerce, I'm blending creative thinking with strategic insights to elevate my work further.

Born on June 13, 2004, I thrive on pushing creative boundaries and turning ideas into reality. If you're ready to bring your vision to life, let's collaborate and create something extraordinary together.

03.5 — Process

Behind
The Work

From a feeling to a finished frame. Scroll sideways through how a piece actually gets made.

  1. 01

    Idea

    It starts with a feeling, not a brief. What should this make people feel?

  2. 02

    Sketch

    Rough shapes, type, layout. Fast and ugly on purpose.

  3. 03

    Design

    Layers, masks, light. Building the frame piece by piece.

  4. 04

    Edit

    Cutting to the beat. Every frame earns its place or goes.

  5. 05

    Motion

    Timing, easing, grain. Where a still image starts to breathe.

  6. 06

    Final

    Export, deliver, move on to the next one.

05 — Questions

FAQthe short version →

Are you available for freelance work?

Yes. Message me on WhatsApp with what you need and I'll get back to you.

What kind of projects do you take on?

Branding and print, photo manipulation and digital art, motion graphics and video editing, and social media campaigns.

Is it just graphic design, or video and 3D too?

All three, genuinely — graphic design, video editing, and 3D art with Blender. Currently leading video editing operations at the Ministry of Youth and Sports while still doing design and motion work on the side.

What software do you work in?

Photoshop, Illustrator, and Premiere Pro day to day, plus Blender for 3D.

What are EYAD Studio and the Store?

Both live at madebyeyad.web.app. The Studio is my free creative suite — image, vector, video, 3D and a film camera, right in your browser. The Store sells heavyweight black clothes, prints and objects I design, made in Cairo in small runs, with delivery all over Egypt and cash on delivery.

What's the fastest way to reach you?

WhatsApp — the button on the Contact section below goes straight to it. Instagram, LinkedIn, and Behance are all linked there too if you'd rather start there.
