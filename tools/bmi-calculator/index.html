What it means: BMI is a quick, widely-used screening number (weight relative to height squared) — it doesn't account for muscle mass, bone density, age, or where fat is distributed, so it's not a diagnosis. Categories follow the WHO's standard adult ranges: under 18.5 underweight, 18.5–24.9 healthy, 25–29.9 overweight, 30+ obese.
