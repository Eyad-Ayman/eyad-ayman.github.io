AI Smart Face Crop

Drop in a portrait and get it re-cropped for every common social size at once — Instagram square, portrait, Story/Reel, and a wide cover — all centered on the actual detected face, not just the middle of the image.

Runs Google's MediaPipe face detector entirely on your device (~230KB, loaded once). No uploading, no account, free.

How it works: the model finds the face's bounding box, then each crop is centered on that box with sensible headroom for that format — a square post, a 4:5 portrait post, a 9:16 Story/Reel, and a 16:9 cover — instead of a plain center-crop that can cut heads off. If more than one face is found, it centers on the one it's most confident about.

Check the red box above the crops actually lands on the face before trusting the results — this is a small, fast on-device model tuned to catch real faces even in candid/lower-quality photos, and that means it can occasionally mistake a busy or textured background (gravel, sand, foliage) for one. If it's off, the photo just isn't a great fit for this tool.
