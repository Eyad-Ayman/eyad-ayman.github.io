Bulk WebP Converter

Drop in as many JPGs/PNGs as you want, convert them all to WebP at once, download individually or as a batch. Runs entirely in your browser — nothing is ever uploaded anywhere.

Drag & drop images here, or

How it works: each image is drawn to an off-screen canvas and re-encoded as WebP with canvas.toBlob() — a standard browser feature, no server, no upload, no size limit beyond what your browser can hold in memory. WebP typically comes out 25–35% smaller than the original JPG/PNG at the same visual quality.
